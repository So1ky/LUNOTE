Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Lunote Inc.

This policy is published in English and Korean. The Korean version is the legally binding text under the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea; the English version is provided for convenience.

Lunote Inc. (“LUNOTE”, “we”) operates the LUNOTE mobile app and the website lunoteapp.com, a concierge service for foreigners living in or moving to Korea. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 14. The service is for adults.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and verify your email address
  • To review your request, prepare a quote and carry out the service you paid for
  • To process payments and refunds and keep the transaction records the law requires
  • To send you service emails (verification codes, quote and payment notices) and reply to support requests
  • To keep the service reliable and safe: detecting abuse, fixing errors, measuring performance
  • To meet legal obligations, including tax and e-commerce record keeping

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Local service partners

Fulfilling a request often means working with a vetted Korean partner (for example a real-estate agent, a clinic or a telecom shop). With your agreement given when you accept a quote, we share only what that partner needs: your name, contact method and the details of the request. Partners may use the information only to carry out your request.

The following companies process personal information on our behalf under contracts that limit them to our instructions.

PayPal, Brevo and Sentry store data outside Korea as shown above. Transfers happen over encrypted connections at the moment the related service is used (a payment, an email, an error report), and are limited to the items each company needs. You may refuse an international transfer by contacting us; however, we cannot process a USD payment, send service emails or operate the sign-in flow without the relevant provider, so refusing may make part of the service unavailable.

Legal requests

We disclose information to courts or authorities only when a law or a valid legal order requires it.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Account details → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, see How to delete your account. Deletion removes or anonymises your personal information as described in section 4. Records held by PortOne or PayPal about a payment, and emails already in our support mailbox, are governed by their retention rules and ours respectively and are not reachable through in-app deletion.

6. Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your personal information, ask us to stop processing it, or withdraw consent at any time. Name, nationality, language and profile photo can be edited in the app. For anything else, email us from the address registered to your account. We respond within 10 days. If a legal retention duty prevents deletion, we tell you which records remain and why. You may also appoint a representative to act for you.

7. Cookies and tracking

The website lunoteapp.com does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The app stores sign-in tokens on your device so you stay signed in; it does not use advertising identifiers.

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (TLS). Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes; verification codes are stored as hashes too.
  • Databases and file storage are not reachable from the public internet; files are served through short-lived signed links.
  • Administrator access requires a separate administrator account, is limited to named staff, and every administrator action is logged and reviewed.
  • Error reports have personal data stripped before they leave our servers.
