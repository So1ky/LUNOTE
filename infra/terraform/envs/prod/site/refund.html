Refund Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Lunote Inc.

This policy is part of the Terms of Service. The Korean version is the legally binding text.

You pay only after you have accepted a quote that states the scope and the price. What you can get back depends on how far the work has gone when you cancel.

1. Refund amounts

SituationRefund
You cancel after paying, before we start the workFull refund, no questions asked
You cancel after we have started the workRefund of the part not yet performed. We deduct the value of work already done and third-party costs already incurred for your request (for example a partner's booking fee), and tell you the breakdown before refunding.
The work is completeNo refund, because the service has been delivered in full
We cannot deliver the agreed scope, or we deliver it materially wrongFull refund, at any stage. If part of the scope was delivered correctly and is useful to you on its own, we may offer a partial refund instead, with your agreement.
Your payment could not be matched to a quote (wrong amount or currency, duplicate payment)Full refund of the unmatched payment

“Starting the work” means we or a partner have begun acting on your request: for example contacting a landlord, booking an appointment, or preparing documents. The request's status in the app changes to “In progress” when work begins.

Under Article 17 of the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce you may withdraw within 7 days of payment. Because our service begins with your express consent when you accept the quote, withdrawal after work has started is limited to the unperformed part as described above.

2. How to request a refund

  1. Email contact [at] lunoteapp [dot] com from the email address registered to your account, and tell us which request it is about (the category and date shown in the app).
  2. We confirm receipt within 2 business days and tell you the refund amount and, if anything is deducted, why.
  3. We issue the refund within 3 business days of confirming the amount.

3. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through our payment provider. We cannot refund to a different account. After we issue it, PayPal or your card issuer may take up to 5–10 further business days to show the money in your account. Currency-conversion differences and fees charged by your bank or PayPal on the original payment are outside our control and are not refunded by us.

4. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, reply to our email and we will review it again with a different staff member. You may also contact the Korea Consumer Agency (kca.go.kr) or open a dispute with your payment provider. Business days are Korean business days (Monday to Friday, excluding Korean public holidays).
