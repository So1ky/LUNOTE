LUNOTE

A concierge for life in Korea.

LUNOTE helps foreigners moving to or living in Korea handle the hard parts — housing, phone plans, banking, healthcare and more — through a simple request-and-quote service with vetted local partners.

The LUNOTE app is in development and will launch on iOS and Android.

What we help with

Settling into Korea means paperwork, phone calls, and appointments — most of them in Korean. Send LUNOTE a request in your own language and we take it from there.

Housing

Finding a place, understanding jeonse and wolse contracts, and getting through move-in day without surprises.

Visa & immigration

Preparing documents, booking immigration office appointments, and keeping track of what's due when.

Hospital

Booking appointments at clinics and hospitals, and helping with communication and insurance paperwork.

Bank

Opening a Korean bank account and getting cards and transfers working.

Telecom

Choosing a phone plan, activating a SIM, and setting up home internet.

Anything else

Can't find the right category? Tell us what you're trying to solve. We research the issue, talk to Korean service providers on your behalf, or connect you with the right professional.

How it works

  1. Send a request

    Choose a category, describe what you need, and tell us how to reach you.

  2. Get a quote

    We review your request and send back a quote with the scope and the price — before you pay anything.

  3. We handle it

    Accept the quote and pay in the app. We take care of the rest with vetted local partners and keep you updated along the way.
